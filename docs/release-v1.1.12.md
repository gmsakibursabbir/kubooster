## Flash Exclusive mode for 90 FPS on PUBG Flash

- **New "Flash Exclusive" graphics option (PUBG Flash only)** - PUBG Flash has its own lightest graphics mode, "FLASH Exclusive", and its save holds a matching switch (`bFlashOnlyQuality`). KuBooster now turns that switch on together with the Super Smooth graphics level and your chosen frame rate, so Flash can run its lightest mode at Extreme+ (90). It is preselected when Flash is chosen; picking any other preset switches it off again.
- **Restore puts the switch back** - Restore resets Flash's switch to its original value along with frame rate and graphics.
- **Diagnostic log** - shows the switch's value on every load, apply and restore.
- **No forced 60 FPS on Flash** - the FPS you pick is kept; the FPS card explains what to do if Flash's meter still stays at 60.

Whether Flash allows 90 depends on the game and the phone: if the in-game FPS meter stays at 60 with Flash Exclusive on, Flash caps that phone at 60 and 60 is the smooth choice.

Shizuku (ADB-level access, no root) required for GFX mode. GFX mode edits PUBG's config and save files, which is detectable by anti-cheat - KuBooster warns explicitly and never guarantees any outcome.
